Souryabrata Mohapatra

Assistant Professor of Economics, Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur

Souryabrata Mohapatra is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at IIT Jodhpur. He completed his PhD in Economics at the University of Auckland Business School.

His research examines climate risks, agricultural transformation, circular systems, energy transitions, and distress migration, particularly in contexts shaped by structural development challenges.

His teaching includes microeconomics, environmental economics, and econometrics. Explore his publications, working papers, projects, and teaching on this academic website.
